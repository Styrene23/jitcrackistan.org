Official symbols of Jitcrackistan



Over the past 2 months, the following symbols have been used to represent Jitcrackistan, its people, and collective pride.



The Coat of Arms


The Coat of Arms of Jitcrackistan

Source: The Government of Jitcrackistan

The Coat of Arms were designed by Daniel Hampson et al. on December 11th of 2025.
The Coat of Arms of Jitcrackistan display the white cat from Sprunki and the Snail Monkey.
The poppy plants signify Jitcrackistan's major export of opioids to other countries for medical use. The lemon is to represent that Jitcrackistan is the #1 exporter of lemons in the world. Jitcrackistan has the world's most reliable and advanced high-speed railway system. The Jitcrackistan picnic team won the global picnic championships 10x in a row in January 2026.



The American Woodcock


The American Woodcock

Source: Rhododendrites on Wikimedia

The American Woodcock is a bird belonging to the Scolopax genus.
The American Woodcock is in North Jitcrackistan over the summer and in South Jitcrackistan over the winter. This bird is symbolic to Jitcrackistan because in the past their migration patterns have been used to determine the season. This bird is under special protection and the intentional killing/harming of the American Woodcock will result in a deduction of 100,000 social credit instead of the normal 10,000 for hunting.
